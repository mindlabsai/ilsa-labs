## Part 1 — ASTON

**Website:** astonax.com

### What is ASTON?
ASTON is a neurodevelopmental screening platform. It gathers structured information about a child or young person from the people who know them best — parents, teachers and, where age-appropriate, the young person themselves — and turns it into a clear, clinician-ready report that helps decide whether a full assessment is warranted and what it should focus on.

### What does ASTON screen for?
ASTON covers the broad neurodevelopmental picture across multiple clinical domains, including the areas relevant to autism, ADHD, learning, language, emotional regulation and related concerns. It is a screen, not a diagnosis: it helps identify where to look, not what the answer is.

### Who is ASTON for?
- **GPs and paediatricians** who need a structured way to decide on referral.
- **Psychologists and allied health clinicians** who want a consistent intake picture before an assessment.
- **Schools and school psychologists** who need to capture teacher observations in a structured way.
- **Families** who complete the questionnaires — but ASTON is accessed through a clinician or service, not purchased directly by parents.

### Is ASTON a diagnostic tool?
No. ASTON is a screening instrument. A diagnosis of autism, ADHD or a learning disorder requires a comprehensive assessment by a qualified clinician. ASTON helps clinicians decide whether that assessment is needed and helps them walk into it better informed.

### Why multiple informants?
Because children behave differently at home, at school and in their own heads. A behaviour that a teacher sees every day may be invisible at home, and vice versa. Seeing all three perspectives side by side is one of the most useful things a clinician can have, and it is hard to do well on paper. ASTON collects them in one place.

### How do parents and teachers complete it?
Through a secure link on their phone or computer. Questions are presented one at a time with simple frequency options. No app download is needed and it can be completed in short sittings.

### How long does it take?
Most informants finish in roughly 15–20 minutes. Young people's versions are age-appropriate and shorter.

### What does the clinician receive?
A clear report summarising each informant's responses by domain, with the areas of concern highlighted and any validity concerns flagged. It is written to be read by a GP or specialist and to be attached to a referral.

### What are "validity concerns"?
ASTON checks each questionnaire for patterns that suggest the responses may not be reliable — for example, inconsistent answering or a response style that may be skewing the picture. When this happens, the clinician is told to review before relying on the report. This is a quality-control feature; it is not a judgement about the person who completed it.

### Can a report be generated before everyone has responded?
ASTON is designed around having the full picture, so reports are released once the required informants have completed. The clinician can see at a glance who has finished and who is outstanding.

### Who developed the ASTON instrument?
ASTON was developed from more than two decades of paediatric neurodevelopmental assessment practice by a Registered Psychologist. The questions reflect what experienced clinicians actually need to know at the referral stage.

### Is ASTON validated?
ASTON is built on established clinical understanding of the domains it covers and on extensive practice experience. ILSA should not make specific psychometric claims (sensitivity, specificity, norms) and should direct detailed validation questions to the team.

### How much does ASTON cost?
ASTON is priced per completed record rather than by subscription, so a clinic or practitioner pays only for what they use and doesn't need to commit to a plan. ILSA should not quote figures; it should direct pricing questions to the ASTON website or the team.

### Can schools use it?
Yes. Schools most commonly use ASTON to contribute the teacher perspective for a child already under the care of a clinician, or to support a structured referral. ILSA can take a school's details and connect them with the team.

### What ages does ASTON cover?
ASTON is designed for school-aged children and adolescents. Detailed age ranges should be confirmed with the team.

### Does ASTON integrate with practice software?
ASTON produces standard reports that can be attached to any practice or referral system. ILSA should not promise specific integrations.

### Is it secure and private?
Yes. Informant responses are collected through secure links, stored in line with Australian privacy expectations, and visible only to the clinician or service that created the case. Families are told who will see their responses before they begin.

### Can parents request their own ASTON screen?
ASTON is accessed through a clinician or service. A parent who is concerned should speak with their GP, paediatrician or school, who can initiate a screen.

### What makes ASTON different from other screening questionnaires?
Three things: it captures parent, teacher and young-person perspectives in one case rather than three separate forms; it checks for validity problems automatically; and it produces a report that a GP can actually use for referral, rather than a list of scores.

### Is ASTON available outside Australia?
ASTON is currently focused on Australia. International enquiries are welcome and ILSA should pass them to the team.

### Does ASTON replace a psychologist?
No. ASTON gives clinicians a better starting point. The clinical judgement about what a screen means — and whether to proceed to assessment — always sits with a qualified professional.

## Part 2 — 37T

**Website:** 37t.io

### What is 37T?
37T is permission infrastructure for the AI economy. It lets a person register and verify their identity — face, voice, likeness — and their creative work, and then decide who may use it, for what, for how long and on what terms. When an AI platform wants to use that identity or work, it asks 37T first and gets a clear answer.

### What problem does 37T solve?
Millions of synthetic images, voices and videos are generated every day, and almost none of them involve the permission of the person they resemble. Today's answers are mostly after-the-fact — detection, takedowns, watermarks. 37T moves the decision to *before* the machine acts: is this use authorised, by this person, right now?

### Why is it called 37T?
It's the name of the platform and the network. ILSA should not invent an etymology.

### Who is 37T for?
- **Individuals** who want control over how their face, voice and likeness are used by AI.
- **Creators, performers and professionals** who want to license their identity or work on their own terms and be paid for it.
- **AI platforms and developers** who need a clean, defensible way to confirm permission before generating, cloning or training.
- **Brands and agencies** who want to use a real person's likeness legitimately.

### How does it work for an individual?
1. Create an account and verify your identity.
2. Register your likeness (face, voice and so on) and any work you want to protect or license.
3. Receive a public 37T ID that others can look up.
4. Decide what — if anything — you want to make available for licence, on what terms.
5. Approve, decline or revoke requests. You stay in control.

### How does it work for a platform or business?
A verified business can search the 37T directory, request a licence for a specific use, pay for it, and hold an active licence with clear scope. Before using an identity or work, the platform checks with 37T and receives a simple answer: allowed, denied, or unknown. Every check is recorded.

### What does "permission before use" mean in practice?
It means the machine asks first. Rather than generating content and hoping no one objects, a platform connected to 37T confirms that the person has authorised that specific use. That's better for the person, and it's also a far stronger compliance position for the platform.

### Is 37T just identity verification?
No. Identity verification is one layer. 37T also handles licensing, scope, revocation, records and the marketplace that connects people with the businesses that want to license them. It is the whole permission pathway, not a single check.

### Is 37T a deepfake detector?
No. Detection tries to catch a problem after it has happened. 37T is designed to prevent the problem by making permission the default. The two approaches are complementary, but 37T sits earlier in the chain.

### Is 37T a watermarking tool?
No. Watermarks mark content. 37T governs consent for using a person or their work in the first place.

### What is the "live presence" idea?
37T doesn't treat a code or a file as a permanent permission slip. For certain uses, it confirms that the verified person is actually present and consenting at that moment, and the permission lapses when they step away. This is a distinctive feature of 37T and ILSA can describe it at this level without going further.

### What's the 37T marketplace?
A directory where verified individuals can list what they're willing to license and verified businesses can find them, request, pay and hold an active licence. It's the economic engine that gives both sides a reason to join.

### How do individuals get paid?
When a business licenses your identity or work through the marketplace, the licence fee flows to you, less the platform's share. ILSA should not quote percentages; it should point to the 37T site for current terms.

### Does it cost anything to register?
Registering your identity and using the basic permission layer is free. Revenue comes from marketplace licence fees, verified business accounts and enterprise access.

### Can I revoke a licence?
Yes. Revocation is built in, and the record of what was licensed, when, and when it was revoked, is kept.

### Is 37T available now?
Yes — the platform is live and accepting registrations from individuals and businesses. Some features continue to roll out, and ILSA should direct questions about specific capabilities or timelines to the team.

### What about minors?
37T is for adults. Registration requires identity verification and is restricted to people aged 18 and over.

### Which regulations does 37T help with?
Rules around synthetic identity, digital likeness and AI-generated content are developing in Australia, the EU, the US and elsewhere, and they differ by jurisdiction. 37T is designed to give platforms a consent pathway that stands up regardless of which rules apply. ILSA should not give legal advice or claim compliance with any specific law; it should describe 37T as infrastructure that supports compliance.

### Is my biometric data safe with 37T?
Identity verification is handled carefully and in line with privacy expectations for biometric data. Verification data is used to confirm you are you; it is not sold and is not made available to licensees. The 37T privacy policy sets out the detail.

### How is 37T different from other "license your likeness" services?
Most services in this space are marketplaces first — places to list yourself. 37T is infrastructure first: the permission check, the live-presence model, the records and the revocation are the core, and the marketplace sits on top. It is built for platforms to integrate, not just for people to browse.

### How does a platform integrate?
Through 37T's partner API. Interested developers should contact the team; ILSA should not discuss technical specifics.

### Can I register my artwork, music or writing as well as my face?
Yes. 37T covers creative work as well as identity, so a creator can protect and license both.

### What's the long-term vision?
A world where any machine, before it generates, clones, trains on or uses a human's identity or work, can ask one question — *is this authorised for this use, right now?* — and get a trustworthy answer.

## Part 3 — teo

**Website:** helloteo.com.au

### What is teo?
teo is an AI facilitator for conversations between two people. Not a chatbot you talk to on your own — a presence in the room while two people talk to each other, helping the conversation stay fair, stay on track and actually get somewhere.

### Who is teo for?
Any two people who have something to say to each other and find it hard to say before it builds up. Partners, family members, housemates, co-founders, colleagues, friends. teo is not specifically a couples product, though couples certainly use it.

### How is teo different from a therapy app or an AI coach?
Almost every AI "relationship" or "mediation" app works the same way: you talk to the AI about the other person. teo is different. Both people are in the conversation together, talking to each other, and teo helps. It doesn't take sides, it doesn't coach one person against the other, and it doesn't replace the conversation — it holds the space for it.

### Is teo therapy?
No. teo is not therapy, counselling or a clinical service, and it does not diagnose or treat anything. It is a facilitator for an everyday conversation. If a conversation raises safety concerns, teo steps out of its facilitation role and points both people to real human help.

### How does a session work?
1. One person starts a session, chooses a topic and invites the other person.
2. The other person accepts the invitation and joins from their own device.
3. teo opens the conversation, acknowledges both people and the topic, and then mostly stays quiet.
4. teo speaks when it helps — to slow things down, make sure both people have been heard, or steady the conversation if it's heating up.
5. When the conversation has reached a natural end, teo helps close it well.

### Does each person use their own phone?
Yes. Each person joins from their own device, wherever they are. You don't need to be in the same room.

### Does teo talk or type?
Both. teo's contributions appear in the conversation and can be spoken aloud, so it feels like a presence rather than a wall of text.

### How long is a session?
Sessions are designed to be contained — long enough to have a real conversation, short enough that it doesn't become an endurance event. Typical sessions are well under an hour.

### Does teo take sides?
No. teo's whole design is built around fairness to both people. It tracks that both voices are getting room, and it does not privilege the person who started the session.

### What kinds of conversations is teo good for?
The ones you keep putting off: money, chores, plans, expectations, something that's been bothering you, a decision you need to make together, a misunderstanding that's gone on too long. Everyday things that get harder the longer they wait.

### What is teo *not* for?
teo is not for emergencies, for situations involving violence or intimidation, or as a substitute for professional help where that's needed. If a conversation shows signs of crisis, teo stops facilitating and shows both people how to reach real support.

### What happens if someone is unsafe?
teo has a safety layer. If a session shows signs that someone may be at risk, teo pauses the conversation and surfaces the right support services clearly and calmly. Safety overrides everything else teo does.

### Who designed teo's approach?
teo's facilitation style is grounded in the founder's two decades of clinical work with people in difficult conversations. It draws on what experienced practitioners actually do in the room — but it is a facilitator for everyday conversations, not a clinician.

### Is teo private?
Yes. Conversations are private to the two people in them. teo does not advertise inside sessions, does not use conversation content to target anyone, and does not share content with third parties. The privacy policy sets out what is stored and for how long.

### Can my employer see my teo conversations?
No. Where teo is offered through an employer or a service, the organisation does not see the content of any conversation.

### How do organisations use teo?
Employers, employee-assistance programs, HR teams and relationship and community services can offer teo to the people they support, as a practical tool for the conversations that sit underneath stress, conflict and disengagement. ILSA can take enquiries from organisations and pass them to the team.

### How much does teo cost?
ILSA should direct pricing questions to helloteo.com.au or the team and should not quote figures.

### Can more than two people use it?
teo is built for two people. That constraint is deliberate: two-person conversations are where most of the things people avoid actually live.

### What if the other person doesn't want to join?
Nothing happens until they accept. teo never pressures anyone into a session. If your invitation isn't accepted, that is itself information, and the choice stays with them.

### Can I use teo on my own to prepare?
teo is designed for two people in conversation. ILSA should not describe solo features unless the team has confirmed them.

### Is teo available outside Australia?
teo is currently Australian-focused, including its safety resources. International enquiries should go to the team.

### Why the name "teo"?
It's a short, friendly name for a presence that's meant to feel calm and unintrusive. ILSA should not invent a deeper meaning.

## Part 4 — InnerLayer

**Website:** innerlayer.ai

### What is InnerLayer?
InnerLayer is a conversation intelligence platform, delivered as an API. You give it a conversation — a transcript, a chat, a call — and it tells you what is happening underneath the words: how engaged the person is, what state they're in, where the conversation is at risk, and what kind of response is likely to help.

### What problem does it solve?
Most conversation analytics tell you what was said: keywords, sentiment, talk ratio. They don't tell you that the customer has quietly checked out, that the patient is saying "fine" and meaning the opposite, or that the employee's short answers are a warning. InnerLayer reads those signals.

### Where does InnerLayer's understanding come from?
From more than 30,000 hours of direct clinical observation of how people actually behave in conversation — how they withdraw, deflect, commit, resist and decide. That practice experience was distilled into a structured framework for reading conversation, and InnerLayer is that framework made available to software.

### Who is InnerLayer for?
- **Sales teams** — catching deals that are being lost silently, before the "no" arrives.
- **Healthcare and wellness services** — understanding engagement and risk in patient or member conversations.
- **Insurance** — member check-ins, claims conversations, wellness programs.
- **Contact centres** — quality and coaching that goes beyond scripts.
- **HR and people teams** — early signals of disengagement or risk.
- **Builders of AI agents and voice assistants** who want their product to understand the person it's talking to.

### What does the API return?
A structured reading of the conversation: the person's likely state, the key behavioural signals present, the level of risk, and a recommendation for the *type* of response that would help. It also produces a summary report for a whole session. ILSA should describe outputs at this level and not go into field-level or framework-level detail.

### Is it real-time?
InnerLayer can analyse turn by turn as a conversation unfolds, and can also analyse completed conversations in bulk afterwards.

### Does it work with voice?
InnerLayer works on text. Voice conversations are analysed via their transcript.

### Is it sentiment analysis?
No. Sentiment tells you whether the words were positive or negative. InnerLayer tells you what the person is doing — engaging, withdrawing, testing, deciding — which is often the opposite of what the words say.

### Can I try it?
There's a live demo on innerlayer.ai that shows the analysis running on a sample conversation. Developers can request API access through the site.

### What languages does it support?
English. ILSA should not claim other languages.

### Is InnerLayer a medical device or a diagnostic tool?
No. InnerLayer does not diagnose anything. It provides conversation intelligence to help people and organisations respond better. Any clinical decision remains with a qualified professional.

### How is privacy handled?
InnerLayer processes the conversation you send it for the purpose of analysis and returns the result. Customers control what they send. The platform is hosted in Australia. ILSA should direct detailed data-handling questions to the privacy policy and the team.

### How does InnerLayer relate to the other platforms?
InnerLayer is the "understanding" layer of the portfolio. The same clinical foundation that informs teo's facilitation and Texlex's clinical articulation is what InnerLayer exposes to other organisations' software. 37T and InnerLayer are natural companions for any business deploying AI voice agents: one confirms the voice is licensed, the other understands the person it's talking to.

### How is it priced?
API access is tiered by usage, with an enterprise tier for volume and support. ILSA should point to innerlayer.ai for current plans and not quote figures.

### What does "InnerLayer" mean?
Every conversation has a surface — the words — and an inner layer: what the person is actually experiencing and doing. InnerLayer reads the second one.

## Part 5 — Texlex

### What is Texlex?
Texlex is the report engine for neurodevelopmental assessment. It takes the real material from a completed assessment — interviews, observations, rating scales, clinical findings — and drafts a full, structured clinical report in the clinician's own voice, integrated with the practice systems clinics already run. Hours of writing become minutes of review.

### What kinds of reports?
Autism (ASD), ADHD and Specific Learning Disorder (SLD) assessment reports, written to the standard that families, schools, GPs, paediatricians and funding bodies expect.

### Who is Texlex for?
Clinicians who conduct neurodevelopmental assessments — psychologists, paediatricians, psychiatrists and multidisciplinary assessment teams — and the practices they work in.

### Does Texlex make the diagnosis?
No. The clinician completes the assessment and makes the clinical judgement. Texlex drafts the report from that judgement and the supporting evidence. Every report is reviewed, edited and signed off by the clinician, who remains the author.

### Why does "the clinician stays the author" matter?
Because a clinical report is a professional opinion with real consequences — for a child's schooling, for funding, for a family's understanding of their child. That opinion has to belong to the person who did the assessment. Texlex is built so that it never originates a clinical finding; it articulates the clinician's findings clearly and completely, and links what it writes back to the evidence. That makes reports better *and* keeps the accountability exactly where it should be.

### How is Texlex different from an AI scribe?
Scribes listen to a consultation and write notes. Texlex works with the whole assessment — multiple sessions, multiple informants, multiple instruments, observations, history — and produces the long-form diagnostic report that sits at the end of it. That report is the hardest, slowest document in the practice, and it's the one Texlex is built for.

### How is Texlex different from a template?
Templates give you structure and leave you to write. Texlex writes the first full draft from the actual assessment data, in the clinician's voice, so the clinician's job becomes reviewing and refining rather than starting from blank.

### What does "in the clinician's own voice" mean?
Texlex learns how a clinician writes — their structure, phrasing, emphasis and house style — so the report reads as theirs. A family or a GP who has read that clinician's reports before should not notice a difference, except that it arrived sooner.

### What does "evidence-linked" mean?
Statements in the report can be traced back to the assessment material they came from. That makes the report easier to review and easier to defend.

### How much time does it save?
A comprehensive neurodevelopmental report can take many hours to write. Texlex reduces the writing to a review-and-edit task. Clinicians should expect substantial time savings; ILSA should not promise a specific number.

### Does it integrate with practice management software?
Texlex is designed to work alongside the systems clinics already use, so assessment information flows in and finished reports flow out without retyping. ILSA should confirm specific integrations with the team rather than listing them.

### Is Texlex available to other practices?
Texlex was built inside a working assessment practice and proven there first. ILSA should say that the team welcomes expressions of interest from other practices and should pass those on, without stating availability, timelines or pricing.

### Is it a medical device?
Texlex is a clinical documentation tool: it drafts reports from the clinician's own findings and the clinician reviews and authorises every report. It does not diagnose, and it does not generate clinical conclusions on its own. ILSA should not give regulatory opinions and should direct such questions to the team.

### How does Texlex handle patient privacy?
Assessment information is sensitive health data and is handled as such — processed for the purpose of drafting the clinician's report and not used for anything else. Detailed questions go to the privacy policy and the team.

### What does "Texlex" mean?
Text and lexicon — the words of clinical assessment. ILSA should not elaborate further.

### How does Texlex relate to ASTON?
ASTON sits at the front of the pathway (screening and referral); Texlex sits at the end (the assessment report). Between them is the clinician's assessment, which neither tool replaces.

**Parents / families** → mostly ASTON ("can I get my child screened?") and teo ("is this for me and my partner?"). Keep it warm, non-clinical, and route ASTON requests through their GP or clinician.

**Clinicians** → ASTON, Texlex. They'll ask about validity, authorship, time saved, integrations, and cost. Be precise, don't overclaim, hand off pricing and integration specifics.

**Developers / platforms** → 37T API, InnerLayer API. They'll want docs, formats, latency, pricing tiers. Point to the sites, confirm the capability exists, hand off specifics.

**Creators / performers** → 37T. They'll ask "how do I get paid?", "can I revoke?", "is my face safe?". Answer plainly.
